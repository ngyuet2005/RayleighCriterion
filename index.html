1為甚麼一點光會變成一個光斑？Why does a point of light become a blob?

光穿過一個圓形孔徑（例如眼睛的瞳孔、相機或望遠鏡的鏡頭）時會發生衍射。所以即使是一個「點」光源，成像後都不是一點，而是一個明亮的中央圓斑，外面圍着一圈圈暗淡的光環，稱為艾里斑（Airy disk）。

When light passes through a circular aperture (the pupil of your eye, a camera or telescope lens) it diffracts. So even a point source is imaged not as a point but as a bright central disk surrounded by faint rings — the Airy disk.

由中心到第一個暗環（第一極小）的角度就是 θmin ≈ 1.22λ/D。孔徑 D 愈細、波長 λ 愈長，光斑就愈大，影像就愈「矇」。

The angle from the centre to the first dark ring (first minimum) is θmin ≈ 1.22λ/D. A smaller aperture D or a longer wavelength λ gives a bigger spot and a blurrier image.

💡 重點：限制分辨能力的是衍射，不是放大倍數。放得再大，兩個重疊的光斑仍然是重疊的。💡 Key idea: what limits resolution is diffraction, not magnification. Magnify two overlapping blobs and they still overlap.

2瑞利判據：怎樣才算「分辨到」兩個物體？The Rayleigh criterion: when are two objects "resolved"?

定義Definition

當一個點光源衍射圖樣的中央極大，剛好落在另一個點光源衍射圖樣的第一極小上時，這兩個物體就剛好能被分辨。

Two point sources are just resolved when the central maximum of one diffraction pattern falls exactly on the first minimum of the other.

換句話說，兩個物體對孔徑所張的角 θ，要大於或等於最小分辨角 θmin：

In other words, the angle θ that the two objects subtend at the aperture must be at least the minimum resolvable angle θmin:

  • θ > θmin：分辨到 — 兩個光斑明顯分開，總強度曲線中間有深的凹位。θ > θmin: resolved — two clearly separate spots; a deep dip in the middle of the total intensity.
  • θ = θmin：剛好分辨 — 中間凹位約為峰值的 74%，勉強看得出是兩點。θ = θmin: just resolved — the dip is about 74% of the peak; you can just tell there are two.
  • θ < θmin：分辨不到 — 兩個光斑重疊成一團，看起來好像只有一個物體。θ < θmin: not resolved — the spots merge into one; it looks like a single object.

📝 瑞利判據是一個約定的標準，不是自然定律。它給了「剛好看得出兩點」一個清楚、可以計算的定義。📝 The Rayleigh criterion is an agreed convention, not a law of nature. It gives "just able to tell two points apart" a clear, calculable definition.

4角解像度 vs 解像度Angular resolution vs resolution

兩個名稱好相似，但一個是角度，一個是長度。考試時先問自己：題目要的是角度（rad）還是距離（m）？

The names are similar, but one is an angle and the other is a length. In exams, first ask: does the question want an angle (rad) or a distance (m)?

5判斷能否分辨：解題步驟Deciding if two objects are resolved: method

  1. 統一單位：nm → ×10−9 m，mm → ×10−3 m，km → ×103 m。Convert units: nm → ×10−9 m, mm → ×10−3 m, km → ×103 m.
  2. 計儀器的極限：θmin = 1.22λ/D。Find the instrument's limit: θmin = 1.22λ/D.
  3. 計物體的角距離：θ = s/L（以弧度表示）。Find the objects' angular separation: θ = s/L (in radians).
  4. 比較：θ ≥ θmin → 分辨到；θ < θmin → 分辨不到。Compare: θ ≥ θmin → resolved; θ < θmin → not resolved.

如題目問「最遠距離」，用 Lmax = s/θmin；問「最少要多大的鏡」，用 Dmin = 1.22λ/θ。

For "maximum distance", use Lmax = s/θmin; for "minimum aperture", use Dmin = 1.22λ/θ.

6例題：車頭燈Worked example: car headlights

一輛汽車的兩盞車頭燈相距 1.5 m。假設瞳孔直徑為 3.0 mm，光的波長為 550 nm。人眼最遠可以在多遠分辨到這兩盞燈？

A car's two headlights are 1.5 m apart. Take the pupil diameter as 3.0 mm and the wavelength as 550 nm. What is the greatest distance at which the eye can resolve the two lights?

角解像度Angular resolutionθmin = 1.22 × (550 × 10−9) / (3.0 × 10−3) = 2.24 × 10−4 rad
剛好分辨時 θ = θmin，即 s/L = θminJust resolved when θ = θmin, i.e. s/L = θminLmax = s / θmin = 1.5 / (2.24 × 10−4) ≈ 6.7 × 103 m ≈ 6.7 km

比 6.7 km 更遠時，兩盞燈的光斑重疊，看起來就像一盞燈。

Beyond about 6.7 km the two spots overlap and the headlights look like a single light.

7怎樣分辨得更好？常見錯誤How to resolve better; common mistakes

✅ 提升分辨能力✅ Improving resolution

  • 增大孔徑 D：大型望遠鏡、大口徑鏡頭（θmin 變細）。Larger aperture D: big telescopes, wide lenses (smaller θmin).
  • 用較短波長 λ：藍光比紅光好；電子顯微鏡用波長極短的電子。Shorter wavelength λ: blue beats red; electron microscopes use electrons with tiny wavelengths.
  • 走近一點（減小 L）：θ = s/L 變大。注意這不會改變 θmin，只會令物體的張角變大。Get closer (smaller L): θ = s/L increases. This does not change θmin; it only makes the objects subtend a larger angle.

⚠️ 常見錯誤⚠️ Common mistakes

  • θ 一定要用弧度，不可用度。θ must be in radians, not degrees.
  • D 是直徑，不是半徑。D is the diameter, not the radius.
  • 忘記把 nm、mm 轉成 m。Forgetting to convert nm and mm to m.
  • 以為 θmin 愈大愈好 — 其實愈細愈好。Thinking a larger θmin is better — smaller is better.
  • 混淆角解像度（rad）與解像度（m）。Mixing up angular resolution (rad) and resolution (m).
